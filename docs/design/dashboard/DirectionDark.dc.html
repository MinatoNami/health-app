Why this is the sketch and not the design
The 73 is invented. There is no reading in this data called readiness — it would be a weighted blend somebody chose, and this project's design rules already refuse one: “No invented composite score. Whoop's 67% has no equivalent here and should not get one — the architecture explicitly forbids the model doing arithmetic, and a 0–100 number would need exactly that.” The dark ground is the defensible half and could be adopted on its own; the number in the middle of it is the half that would make the dashboard say something it cannot know.
