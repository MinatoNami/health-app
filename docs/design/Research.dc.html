All of them
A score with no error bar
Whoop says 67%. It does not say the watch was off for two of the seven nights. Every one of these apps hides how much to trust the number.
The opening: this is the gap.
The thesis
Sell the honesty
This app already grades its own confidence, counts valid days, and reports a metric that stopped as stopped rather than as zero. Nobody else does. Make that the visible product, not an implementation detail.
Deliberately not copied
A 0–100 composite score — it would require inventing a number this system has no basis for, and the whole architecture refuses to let the model do arithmetic. Streaks and rings, which punish rest. Ready/Not-ready verdicts, which are a clinical claim in a friendly coat.
